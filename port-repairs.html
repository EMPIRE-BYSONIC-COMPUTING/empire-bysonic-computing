Reliable Power and Connectivity Fixes

A broken charging port can make a good laptop unusable. Empire Bysonic Computing provides component-level port repairs across the Southern Suburbs, Cape Town, and Western Cape.

Signs of a Damaged Port

  • Loose charging connection
  • No power delivery from a verified charger
  • Intermittent USB or HDMI connectivity
  • Broken plastic, bent pins, or burn marks

Ports We Repair

  • DC jacks and USB-C Power Delivery ports
  • USB-A, USB-C, Thunderbolt, and HDMI connectors
  • 3.5mm headphone and microphone jacks

We can desolder damaged connectors and install new factory-spec ports directly on the board where appropriate.
