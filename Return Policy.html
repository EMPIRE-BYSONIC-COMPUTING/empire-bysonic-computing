Return, Repair & Refund Policy

1. Computer Repair Services & Labor

  • Diagnostic Fees: All diagnostic and evaluation fees are non-refundable. This fee covers the time, expertise, and testing equipment used to identify the problem.

2. Hardware and Parts Returns

If you purchased hardware components, accessories, or custom builds directly from us:

  • Condition for Change-of-Mind: To receive a full refund or exchange on non-defective parts, items must be unopened, unused, and in their original packaging with all documentation included.

3. Exclusions (Non-Returnable Items)

  • Software & Digital Licenses: Once a software key is activated, registered, or a digital product is downloaded, it is 100% non-refundable.
  • Special-Order Parts: Parts custom-ordered specifically for your unique machine cannot be returned for a change of mind.

4. Limited Service Warranty

We back our labor and supplied parts with a 90-day Limited Warranty starting from the date of collection:

  • What is Covered: This warranty strictly covers the specific hardware repair and parts replaced during the initial service.
  • What Voids the Warranty: The warranty is immediately voided if the device shows signs of physical damage, liquid spills, tampering by another party, or power surge damage after leaving our shop.
  • Software Exclusions: We cannot warrant software-related fixes. Issues resulting from viruses, malware, user configuration changes, or operating system updates after the repair are not covered.

5. Data Liability Disclaimer

  • Customer Responsibility: It is the customer's sole responsibility to back up all data, files, and software before leaving a device with us for repair.
