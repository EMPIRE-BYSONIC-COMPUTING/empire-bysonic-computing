A cracked screen or damaged bezel does not mean you need a new laptop. Empire Bysonic Computing provides screen and bezel replacement for major laptop brands across the Southern Suburbs, Cape Town, and Western Cape.

Common Screen Issues We Fix

  • Cracked or smashed glass
  • Flickering displays
  • Dead pixels and lines
  • Black screens and backlight faults
  • Damaged bezels and hinges

Our Repair Process

  1. Inspect the display assembly and confirm the fault.
  2. Source a compatible OEM or equivalent replacement panel.
  3. Install the panel and route the display cables safely.
  4. Test colour, brightness, and hinge movement.
